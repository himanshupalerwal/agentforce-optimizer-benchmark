---
title: What Your Order Status Means
urlName: order-status-meanings
---
Processing: we received your order and are getting it ready to ship.

Shipped: your order has left our warehouse. The expected delivery date is the carrier's estimate.

Delivered: the carrier marked your order as delivered. If you cannot find it, check around your home and with neighbors, then contact us within 7 days.

Cancelled: the order was cancelled. You were not charged, or your refund is on its way.

To check an order, give us your order number, which starts with RO-, and the email address on the order. For your privacy, we share order details only when both match.
