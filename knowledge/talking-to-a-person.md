---
title: Talking to a Person
urlName: talking-to-a-person
---
You can ask to talk to a person at any time, and we will connect you with our customer care team.

Our team is available Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern Time. Outside those hours, we reply by email within one business day.

If a product caused an injury, damaged property, or raised a safety concern, we connect you with a person right away. Please stop using the product until we have spoken with you.
