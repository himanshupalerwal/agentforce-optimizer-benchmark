---
title: "Granite Hiking Boots: Fit and Care"
urlName: granite-hiking-boots
---
The Granite Hiking Boots are mid-height leather boots with a waterproof, breathable membrane and a lugged rubber outsole.

They run true to size. If you are between sizes, choose the larger size to leave room for thick hiking socks.

Wear them around the house for a few days before a long hike, so they can break in.

To clean them, brush off dried mud, rinse with lukewarm water, and let them air dry away from direct heat. Treat the leather with a waterproofing conditioner every few months.
