---
title: "Summit 2 Tent: Specs and Setup"
urlName: summit-2-tent
---
The Summit 2 Tent sleeps two people. It is a three-season, freestanding tent that weighs 4 lb 2 oz with stakes and rainfly.

It has two doors and two vestibules for storing packs and boots.

Setup takes about 10 minutes: clip the tent body to the two color-coded poles, then drape the rainfly over and buckle it at each corner.

It is not designed for snow loads or winter camping. Always dry the tent fully before you store it, to prevent mildew.
