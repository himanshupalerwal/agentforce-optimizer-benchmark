urlName: shipping-options

We ship to addresses in the United States only.

Orders leave our warehouse within 1 to 2 business days.

Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days. It is free on orders over $75, and $5.95 otherwise.

Expedited shipping takes 2 to 3 business days and costs $14.95.

You get a tracking email when your order ships. Delivery dates are estimates and can slip during holidays and severe weather.
