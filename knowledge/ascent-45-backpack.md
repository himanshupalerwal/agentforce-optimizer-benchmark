---
title: "Ascent 45 Backpack: Fit and Capacity"
urlName: ascent-45-backpack
---
The Ascent 45 holds 45 liters, enough for one to three nights on the trail. The pack weighs 3 lb 4 oz.

It fits torso lengths from 16 to 21 inches. Move the shoulder harness up or down to match yours. The hip belt should sit on top of your hip bones and carry most of the weight.

It includes a rain cover, stored in the bottom pocket, and a sleeve for a hydration reservoir of up to 3 liters.
